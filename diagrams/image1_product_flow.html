Agentic RAG — Root Cause Analysis Tool

How it works, end to end — a tool that watches your metrics and explains problems on its own

⚠
BREACH
1
🎛️

Set Up

You choose which product metrics to track and set a guardrail — a safe limit — for each one.

2
📡

Monitor

The tool watches your live dashboard every 10 seconds, checking each metric against its guardrail.

3
🔎

Investigate

The instant a guardrail breaks, the AI investigates on its own:

  • Recalls past incident reports
  • Searches the web for outside factors
  • Picks the right analysis method
4
📄

Report

A clear, structured root-cause report appears in your browser — with a live "thinking" animation. Send it by email in one click.

🔁 Repeats every 10 seconds while all metrics stay healthy
Why “agentic”?
Once you hit start, the tool decides when to act and gathers its own context — no one has to prompt it. It notices the problem, investigates, and writes the explanation automatically.
